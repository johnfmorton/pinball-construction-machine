# Audio sources

Candidate sounds for the Phase 1 sample kit. Every sound that ships must be listed here with its source and license, checked on the sound's own page (Freesound licenses are per sound, not per pack). Vetted October 18, 2026.

The Bronco recordings ([pack](https://freesound.org/people/schafferdavid/packs/25508/)) were made with two Neumann KM 184 mics in XY, the best quality found. relwin's [pack](https://freesound.org/people/relwin/packs/10675/) has more electromechanical sounds (score reels, bells, spinning disc); check each sound's license before use.

Gaps: a clean rolling-ball loop, rubber/post hits, slingshots, drop targets, rollover switches. Plan: cut these from the Bronco full-game recording.

Rejected:
- [GameBoy "Pinball sounds"](https://freesound.org/people/GameBoy/packs/36080/): CC BY 4.0 (attribution required) and 8-bit / 11 kHz.
- [Rolling Ball Loop](https://freesound.org/people/SquirmTheVerm/sounds/260778/) and [Rolling ball](https://freesound.org/people/ChrisGrundlingh/sounds/765635/): CC0, but a bowling ball on hardwood and a heavy ball, not a steel ball on a playfield.
